## Scan design

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MS2 | 2 | precedes any MS1 -> `ms1scan` = 0 -> **null** (source of the sentinel); no `RTINSECONDS` -> `rt` = **0.0, not null** |
| 2 | MS1 | 3 | precursor-lookup conflict: closest peak is NOT the most intense |

## The precursor-lookup conflict (scan 3 -> MS1 scan 2)

`precmz` = 500.0, tolerance 20 ppm -> window +/- 0.01

| MS1 peak m/z | intensity | distance from precmz | in window? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 499.99609375 | 1000.0 | 0.00390625 | yes |
| 500.0078125 | 5000.0 | 0.0078125 | yes |
| 600.0 | 9000.0 | 100.0 | no |

**Correct answer: `ms1_i` = 1000.0, `ms1_precmz` = 499.99609375** (the CLOSEST peak).
An implementation that takes the most intense peak in the window returns 5000.0 /
500.0078125 and is WRONG. `ms1_base_peak_i` = 9000.0 -- the max over the WHOLE MS1
scan, including the 600.0 peak outside the window.
